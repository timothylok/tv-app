# AI Channels - user manual

Open **AI Channels** from the Android TV home screen. It opens on a short menu for the channel you watched last (AI News TV). New episodes are made twice a day, at about 8 am and 2 pm New Zealand time.

A thin line at the top left is always on: the episode (named by the New Zealand time it was made, for example "Today 8:10 am"), when the channel was last updated (New Zealand time) and how much of the free AI allowance is left..

## Home menu
| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Play latest episode | Starts the newest episode from the beginning |
| Continue watching | Shown only when you have a part-watched episode: carries on where you stopped |
| All episodes | The list of every episode still available (about two days), newest first |
| Watch live now | Shown only while an episode is on air: joins it at the live point, like TV |

The highlighted button is the one you most likely want: **Play latest** when there is a new episode, otherwise **Continue watching** if you stopped part-way, otherwise **Play latest** or **All episodes**. The menu updates by itself when a new episode arrives.

## Episode list
Episodes are named by when they were made in New Zealand time ("Today 8:10 am", "Yesterday 2:21 pm", "18 Nov 10:40 pm"), never by a code. Each row shows that time, the length and **New**, **N% watched** or **Watched**. Up/down to move, OK to play (a part-watched episode carries on where you stopped). Back closes the list and changes nothing.

## While an episode plays
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| OK or D-pad up | Show the controls: Pause/Play, Previous (older), Next (newer), More episodes, Exit. They hide after 6 seconds |
| Play/Pause | Pause or carry on at once. Pausing shows the pause menu: Resume, Choose episode, Play latest, Exit playback |
| D-pad left / right | Back / forward 10 seconds |
| D-pad down | Episode details and whether a newer one exists |
| Back | "Leave episode?": Save and exit, Keep watching, Choose another episode. Your place is never lost |
| Menu / Guide | The episode list |
| Channel up / down | Switch to live TV on the next / previous channel |
| Home | Leave the app; the episode pauses and your place is saved |

Your position is saved as you watch (every 10 seconds, on pause, on Back and on Home), also if the box restarts. Within the last 20 seconds an episode counts as watched.

When an episode ends, nothing starts by itself. You choose: **Play next episode**, **Play latest**, **Replay**, **Choose episode** or **Return to menu**. If a newer episode came out while you watched, the screen says so and offers it first.

## Live TV
Channel up/down (or **Watch live now** on the home menu) plays what is on air right now, in sync on every TV. Channel and D-pad up/down switch channel, number keys jump to a channel, OK shows what is on and what is next, D-pad right opens the episode list, Back returns to the home menu. If nothing is on air you go back to the home menu with "Nothing is live right now".

## Messages
- **No internet connection. Trying again...**: the app retries by itself.
- **Playback could not start**: choose Try again, Choose another episode or Exit.

Back on the home menu leaves the app. While a menu is on screen and nothing plays, the TV's screensaver may start.
